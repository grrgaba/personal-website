How your support helps keep this site and my projects going.

Hi — I'm Nico. If you're wondering why you should support my work, here are a few ways your contribution helps:

  • Sustaining independent work: Small donations help cover hosting, tools, and development time so I can keep these utilities and write-ups free and ad-free.
  • Fueling new projects: Support lets me explore new side projects, build useful tools, and experiment with hardware and software that I then share publicly.
  • Quality and maintenance: Ongoing contributions make it possible to fix bugs, update content, and keep the site secure and fast.
  • Community value: Many pages here started as problems I solved for myself — supporting me helps turn those solutions into resources others can reuse.

If you value clear write-ups, practical tools, and small projects that scratch real itches, your support helps ensure this work continues. Even a small, regular contribution makes a big difference.

If you'd like to support, visit the Support Me page for options (PayPal, Square, store links) — thank you.
