❓ How to get your rosters from Yahoo or ESPN

Both sites show each team's roster on its own page, so the simplest route is to copy the player names from each one:

  1. Open your league and click into each team's roster page.
  2. Copy just the player names, not the stats columns.
  3. In the box above, type the team name on its own line, then paste that team's players underneath, one per line.
  4. Leave a blank line, then repeat for the next team.

Example:

Team Alpha
Nikola Jokic
Jayson Tatum

Team Beta
Luka Doncic
Victor Wembanyama

You can also paste a spreadsheet with a team column and a player column, or write Team Alpha: Nikola Jokic, Jayson Tatum on one line. Slot prefixes like PG: or BN - are removed automatically. Each line should be only the player's name: extra text like DEN - C stops the match, and that player will show up as excluded in the results notice.

ℹ️ Notes & data sources

This tool runs locally in your browser and does not upload your pasted rosters.

Projection data: 2026–27 projections are included for every player in the attached league roster, using availability-adjusted games played. Other players in the CSV without a current projection use clearly labeled 2025–26 per-game actuals as a baseline instead. Any players missing from the CSV are listed below the matchup table.

Download the player stats CSV · Sources: Stocks & Buckets 2026–27 projections, Hashtag Basketball, and Basketball-Reference.

Counting stats are estimated by multiplying each player's per-game rate by projected/actual games played. FG% and FT% are weighted by attempts. Lower TO wins.
