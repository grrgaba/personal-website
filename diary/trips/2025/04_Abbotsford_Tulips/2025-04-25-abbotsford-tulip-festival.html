April 25, 2025

Abbotsford Tulip Festival

We left early in the morning, before the day was fully awake, with Nico, Sarah, Tita Lani, and Tita Lani’s friend. The tulips were already glowing in the cool spring air, and the whole field felt like a color story waiting to be walked through.

Flower notes

Rows of hot pink, red, orange, white, and yellow tulips stretched out in every direction, with bright blossoms and a few flowering trees at the edges.

Pink tulips Red tulips Orange tulips Yellow tulips White tulips

What we did

We got there early, wandered the rows, took a bunch of photos, and let the field do the rest. It was one of those simple days that somehow feels huge in memory.

There’s something about tulips first thing in the morning that makes everything feel lighter. The colour is so intense it almost feels unreal, and the mountains in the background made the whole field look even more cinematic. Pink and crimson rows stretched into the distance, while the orange and yellow patches kept the whole thing feeling warm and bright.

We were moving slow, taking photos, laughing, and just soaking in the mood. It was one of those days where no one was rushing — we were just enjoying the flowers, the weather, and being out together before the rest of the day got loud.

Getting there was already a moment of its own — I drove a Tesla for the first time. Smooth, quiet, and kind of surreal in the best way. The drive down matched the whole vibe of the day.
