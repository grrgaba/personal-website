September 2019 · with Dad

Japan

A short trip, just the two of us. We woke up and were out by nine. The first day was Tokyo, Shin-Okubo to Shinjuku, and the day I bought my first pair of Jordans. Yellow. The next day was Odaiba, then Asakusa. After that, Hiroshima, and Kyoto.

Out by nine

Tokyo, Shin-Okubo to Shinjuku

We woke up and left around nine. Dad had gone ahead, so the day started where I caught him: Shin-Okubo, then the fish market, a temple and the station statue, Shibuya, Harajuku, food at Yoyogi, and Shinjuku to close it. Somewhere on that walk I bought my first pair of Jordans. Yellow.

Shin-Okubo

Korea town. Dad was already on the sidewalk with a friend, which is not what I expected to walk into at the start of Tokyo.

Samurai in the street, working the crossing in full armor. A rugby crew stopped us because they wanted a picture with the armor and with us. The flag says Samoa.

We ate. Hot pot, small plates, the two of us at the table.

The next day

Odaiba, then Asakusa

Odaiba first. The bridge, the little Statue of Liberty, the Gundam, and teamLab. Asakusa after that, under the big red lantern.

Asakusa

South

Hiroshima

The Atomic Bomb Dome is still standing, the steel frame of the roof open to the sky. I did not have much to say in front of it.

The night, then the morning

Kyoto

We got in at night and ended up at a table with people we had just met. In the morning it was Fushimi Inari, the torii going up the hill. That afternoon we flew home.
